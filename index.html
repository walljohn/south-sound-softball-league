01 / The League

Softball, the way
the South Sound plays it.

Meet the teams ↗

The South Sound Softball League is a 12U summer league for Tacoma and Pierce County. Games are played on weeknights at Foss, Lincoln, Hunt, and Salish Field. No league fee, and weekends kept open for tournaments. It is run by the coaches whose teams play in it.

How it works

STEP 01

There is no league fee.

Teams keep their budgets for their own programs.

STEP 02

Each team hosts its home games.

The home team covers umpires and field time for the games it hosts, and every team carries its own field insurance.

STEP 03

Games are midweek.

Games are played on weeknights through the summer. Weekends stay open for tournaments.

STEP 04

Schedule and standings live here.

Both are posted on this site and kept current through the season.
